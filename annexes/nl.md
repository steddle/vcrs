# Annex NL: the Netherlands (WAMCA)

VCRS 0.1.0-beta.5. Draft for consultation. Not legal advice. Law and rulings as read on 19, 21, 23 and 27 October 2026: statutes from the BWB, rulings from rechtspraak.nl, each quote checked against the publisher's text.

## 1. The regime

A collective action is brought under art. 3:305a BW and art. 1018b to 1018m Rv (the WAMCA, in force since 1 January 2020) by a stichting or vereniging whose statutes cover the interests. Ad hoc and commercially initiated foundations may sue ('De WAMCA biedt uitdrukkelijk ruimte voor ad hoc organisaties en commerciële organisaties', Rb Amsterdam 27 May 2026, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2026:6440, ro. 6.47); who started the case invites critical review, not a bar (Rb Noord-Holland 23 July 2025, ECLI:NL:RBNHO:2025:8944, ro. 4.32).

The foundation must be one that 'voldoende representatief is, gelet op de achterban en de omvang van de vertegenwoordigde vorderingen' (art. 3:305a lid 2 BW). The Hoge Raad reads this as asking whether the foundation 'de steun heeft van een voldoende groot deel van de totale groep personen voor wie zij opkomt' (HR 17 July 2026, ECLI:NL:HR:2026:1198, ro. 3.3.5). The statute and the Hoge Raad set no number (ro. 3.3.6).

The WAMCA governs a claim filed on or after 1 January 2020 only so far as it concerns events on or after 15 November 2016. For earlier events the conditions in force before 1 January 2020 still apply, 'voor zover de rechtsvordering betrekking heeft op een gebeurtenis of gebeurtenissen die heeft of hebben plaatsgevonden voor 15 november 2016' (art. 119a lid 2 Overgangswet nieuw Burgerlijk Wetboek; HR ro. 3.1.1). A claim that covers events on both sides of the date is split: 'een splitsing van de rechtsvordering moet plaatsvinden' (HR 25 September 2026, ECLI:NL:HR:2026:1551, ro. 3.2.4). The events are the facts the claim rests on, as the foundation pleads them and the defendant contests them (ro. 3.2.5), sorted for each claim against each defendant (ro. 3.3.3). Only for 'een reeks van gebeurtenissen die zowel vóór als na 15 november 2016 plaatsvinden' does the explanatory note to the amendment, as ro. 3.2.7 quotes it, apply the law in force at the last of them, and the Hoge Raad finds such a series only where the facts 'zozeer met elkaar zijn verweven dat', in determining the applicable regime, 'geen splitsing in afzonderlijke gebeurtenissen kan worden gemaakt' (ro. 3.2.7). Before that ruling, the Hof Den Haag treated a failure to inform investors from 2014 to 2020 'als één voortdurende gebeurtenis' and, by analogy with the note, applied the WAMCA to the whole claim (Hof Den Haag 23 December 2025, ECLI:NL:GHDHA:2025:2738, ro. 6.43 and 6.44). Titel 14A Rv, which sets the stages in sections 2 and 3, governs the WAMCA part alone: 'deze titel alleen van toepassing is voor zover de aan de rechter voorgelegde rechtsvordering wordt beheerst door de WAMCA' (ro. 3.2.6). The earlier part claims no damages in money: 'Zij kan niet strekken tot schadevergoeding te voldoen in geld' (art. 3:305a lid 3 BW as in force until 31 December 2019). The split does not apply to a claim for the collective interests of consumers under the Representative Actions Directive filed on or after 25 June 2023 (art. 119a lid 3); whether titel 14A Rv then governs the earlier events too is unsettled (section 8).

## 2. Who is bound, and how they join

- **Residents: opt-out.** Everyone in the narrowly defined class is bound unless they opt out by written statement to the court registry within the term the court sets after the exclusive representative is appointed (art. 1018f lid 1 Rv). Courts adapt the form, by e-mail to the court for instance (Rb Amsterdam 19 March 2025, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2025:1859, ro. 3.18).
- **People living abroad: opt-in.** They are bound only by a written statement to the court registry, within the court's term, that they consent to being represented (art. 1018f lid 5 Rv). In a consumer claim under the Representative Actions Directive filed on or after 25 June 2023 they must also state that their interests are not represented in a collective or individual action on the same events against the same defendant in another EU or EEA state (art. 1018f lid 6 Rv); a claim filed before that day keeps titel 14A Rv as it stood, without lid 6 (art. VI Implementatiewet richtlijn representatieve vorderingen voor consumenten). Outside such claims the court may apply opt-out to them on a party's request (lid 5, last sentence). Both statements are made only after the appointment. Whether a person lives abroad can be answered on a date the court fixes (NL-15). A person abroad belongs to the narrowly defined group the foundation acts for (lid 5); whether their support counts before they are bound is unsettled (section 8), and this standard counts it (NL-15).
- **Joining the foundation** is not a statutory act. It is evidence of support, and not a condition of being bound: '(behoudens een tijdige opt-out) alle personen die behoren tot de groep voor wie de aangewezen exclusieve belangenbehartiger opkomt, door de uitspraak worden gebonden' (HR ro. 3.3.5; art. 1018f lid 1 Rv). Nor is it needed to show support: 'Actieve aanmelding voor de collectieve vordering of lidmaatschap van een belangenorganisatie zijn echter niet vereist om te kunnen aannemen dat sprake is van steun van een voldoende groot deel van de totale groep' (HR ro. 3.3.6). The foundation's website must say how to join and how to leave (art. 3:305a lid 2 sub d 9° BW), so joining is revocable.

## 3. When the register is tested

| Stage | What it asks of the register | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Writ | A precise class definition and how representativeness is met | art. 1018c lid 1 sub b and d Rv |
| Admissibility | Support relative to the whole class, the size of the claims, attribution to class members, a verifiable count, an accurate sign-up flow. Tested as things stand when the court decides | art. 1018c lid 5 Rv; HR ro. 3.2.4, 3.3.5; Rb Amsterdam 2026:6440 ro. 6.14 |
| Exclusive representative | Group size and claim value weigh in the choice between competing claimants | art. 1018e lid 1 Rv |
| Notice | The register supplies the known persons who get a letter, unless the court decides otherwise | art. 1018f lid 3 Rv |
| After appointment | The court may re-test the exclusive representative's admissibility ('een doorlopende controle kan uitvoeren'), but need not: 'De rechtbank houdt geen doorlopend toezicht op de nakoming van in dit verband gestelde vereisten (hier: het waarborgvereiste)'. A mass opt-out can end the case | HR ro. 3.2.4; Rb Amsterdam 10 January 2024, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2024:83, ro. 2.7; art. 1018f lid 1 Rv |
| Distribution | The class minus opt-outs, plus people abroad who opted in, bound once the ruling is final, less anyone who could not have known of their damage at the notice and later declares they will not be bound; per-person proof of membership at the latest here | art. 1018i lid 1, 1018j lid 1, 1018k lid 1 and 2 Rv; Rb Amsterdam 25 September 2024, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2024:5972, ro. 3.5 |

Where a claim is split (section 1), these stages apply to its WAMCA part, and the register states class size and coverage for each part (NL-16).

**Reference date.** The court of appeal judges 'naar de toestand zoals die zich voordoet ten tijde van zijn beslissing' (HR ro. 3.2.2, applied to the WAMCA in ro. 3.2.5), and in judging admissibility 'ook rekening kan worden gehouden met na het uitbrengen van de dagvaarding gewijzigde feiten en omstandigheden' (ro. 3.2.4). The register states a reference date shortly before each oral hearing and regenerates on the court's request. The Rechtbank Amsterdam worked from a count of 1 October 2024 for a hearing of 22 October 2024 (Rb Amsterdam 15 January 2025, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2025:313, ro. 6.8 and 8.4).

## 5. Recommended items

- **Documentary proof of membership per record (grade B).** Courts mostly leave actual membership to the damages stage (Rb Amsterdam 2024:5972 ro. 3.5), but demanded it at admissibility for an investor class whose membership turned on records the foundation could have collected (Rb Noord-Holland 2025:8944 ro. 4.42 and 4.43), and accepted a per-participant overview of invoices and contracts with identities withheld from the defendant (Rb Amsterdam 25 October 2023, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2023:6683, ro. 2.7 and 2.8).
- **Confirmed sign-ups beside raw sign-ups.** The Rechtbank Rotterdam recorded both figures (Rb Rotterdam 28 May 2025, ECLI:NL:RBROT:2025:6254, ro. 6.34: SDBN's 9 421 forms, 4 186 confirmed by e-mail).
- **The cross-section.** The Hoge Raad names whether the people who joined 'een evenwichtige afspiegeling vormen van de totale groep' as a way to infer support of the whole (ro. 3.3.6).
- **The auditor's check at Level 3.** An accountant who examined the operator on site and was shown the results of queries on its databases answered the objection that the count came from exports (Rb Amsterdam 2025:313 ro. 8.5.2). An expert with a bailiff present, making test sign-ups and examining the databases, carried SOMI's count (Rb Amsterdam 2026:6440 ro. 6.19). An accountant who only watched the foundation generate the list was accepted as 'minimaal' (Rb Amsterdam 2025:1859 ro. 3.3), before the Hoge Raad raised the bar.
- **A report under Standaard 4400N.** It puts the burden on the defendant to say concretely what is wrong: 'Een algemeen verwoord vermoeden is ontoereikend' (Rb Amsterdam 2025:313 ro. 8.5.1).
- **Endorsements from organisations with a track record**, recorded apart from the count (core 6.6). The Hoge Raad lets them weigh (ro. 3.3.9); they carried SOMI (Rb Amsterdam 2026:6440 ro. 6.24) and their absence counted against SDBN (Rb Rotterdam 2025:6254 ro. 6.37: 'Dergelijke steunbetuigingen kunnen met name van belang zijn in gevallen waarin er in absolute en relatieve zin een beperkte achterban is.').
- **An explicit mandate per record for a damages claim based on the AVG.** Whether art. 80 AVG requires one is left to the merits (HR ro. 3.4.5), and the Court of Justice may decide whether the WAMCA's requirements apply to such claims at all (C-523/25, pending).
- **A consultation channel** the board uses before settling. It counts toward the safeguards (Hof Den Haag 2025:2738 ro. 6.87; Claimcode 2019, principe V.7).

## 6. Recognised identification

- **Identity, grade A**: iDIN (bank identification) and eHerkenning (for legal persons), beside notified eIDAS means. `nl.json` lists them. DigiD is not available to private parties.
- **Outside sources, grade B**: the RDW vehicle register for vehicle classes; the KvK Handelsregister for business classes.

## 7. The level a typical action needs

A recommendation, not a court's rule.

- **Level 2, Identified, is the floor for a damages action.** Named, contactable, deduplicated people who affirmed membership and support, coverage against the class and its spread, recomputed by an auditor from the source records. Rb Amsterdam accepted counts against the lower test the Hoge Raad rejected, 'een niet te verwaarlozen aantal personen' (stated in 2024:5972 ro. 3.3 and 2025:313 ro. 8.3; a count accepted on it in 2025:1859 ro. 3.3), so no share accepted before 17 July 2026 is safe.
- **Level 3, Verified**, when the register will be fought over: a competing claimant in the window of art. 1018d Rv, a defendant attacking the count, a class of millions where the share will be small, a class whose membership turns on records, or a second chance after an adjournment.
- **Level 1, Registered**, matches the floor accepted in Rb Amsterdam 2025:1859, which predates the Hoge Raad's ruling.

## 8. Unsettled

- Whether titel 14A Rv governs a claim under the Representative Actions Directive filed on or after 25 June 2023 for events before 15 November 2016. Art. 119a lid 3 Overgangswet nieuw Burgerlijk Wetboek lifts the split for art. 3:305a BW, but the legislature left the transitional rule for titel 14A Rv as it stood: 'Artikel II van deze wet is van toepassing op gedingen die aanhangig zijn gemaakt op of na het tijdstip van het in werking treden van de wet en die betrekking hebben op een gebeurtenis of gebeurtenissen die heeft of hebben plaatsgevonden op of na 15 november 2016' (art. III, onderdeel 2, WAMCA), and HR 2026:1551 ro. 3.2.6 reads the two rules alike. The explanatory memorandum to lid 3 points the other way without naming art. III: the Directive applies to actions filed on or after 25 June 2023, 'ongeacht wanneer de gebeurtenissen hebben plaatsgevonden' (Kamerstukken II 2024/25, 36638, nr. 3, on art. XIII, onderdeel A). No ruling decides it.
- No published ruling applies HR 2026:1198 to representativeness yet; Rb Amsterdam found Milieudefensie representative on 9 September 2026 without citing it (ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2026:9098, ro. 3.13). The Hof Den Haag has not decided the referred TPC case.
- Whether support from people abroad counts before any of them is bound. HR ro. 3.3.5 bases the test on the binding effect of the judgment; art. 1018f lid 5 Rv counts people abroad in the group the foundation acts for. No ruling decides it. This standard counts their support and shows it apart (NL-15).
- C-523/25 (SDBN v Amazon) asks whether the Netherlands may add admissibility requirements to GDPR claims. If not, the representativeness test does not apply to privacy damages actions.
- Whether the representative may file the opt-ins of people abroad as their agent, or file them in bulk. The Rechtbank Amsterdam held that a person can in principle be represented in an opt-out: 'het uitbrengen van een opt-out verklaring een rechtshandeling is en dat de wet bepaalt dat personen zich bij het verrichten daarvan in beginsel kunnen laten vertegenwoordigen, al dan niet door middel van volmachtverlening' (Rb Amsterdam 2024:2479 ro. 3.16). No ruling applies this to the opt-in of art. 1018f lid 5 Rv, to the representative as agent, or to filing in bulk.
- The cabinet's response to the WODC evaluation (Kamerstukken I 2026/27, 34608, C, 12 October 2026) proposes no change to the WAMCA, defers on representativeness to the courts, and plans to explore with the Raad voor de rechtspraak how to make the central register for collective actions more complete, accessible and searchable. The WODC studies funding and margins next.
- Where the court applies opt-out to people abroad (art. 1018f lid 5 Rv, last sentence), the export states no term in `opt_in_terms`, a record abroad counts without a statement, and the conformance statement names the court's decision.
