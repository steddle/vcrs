# Annex BE: Belgium (action en réparation collective)

VCRS 0.1.0-beta.6. Draft for consultation. Not legal advice. Law and rulings as read on 19 and 21 October 2026 on Justel, the Moniteur belge, the Cour constitutionnelle, the Chambre des représentants, the SPF Economie and the Conseil central de l'économie.

Written by Holly Thackwray and Mischa Sigtermans for Steddle. Not yet reviewed by counsel admitted in Belgium.

## 1. The regime

A collective damages action is an action en réparation collective (rechtsvordering tot collectief herstel) under Livre XVII, Titre 2 of the Code de droit économique (CDE), arts. XVII.35 to XVII.70, in the consolidated text to 4 August 2026. French and Dutch are both official: this annex quotes the statute in French, and the Dutch stands at the same article.

The law of 21 April 2024, which transposes Directive (EU) 2020/1828, governs the actions introduced from its entry into force on 10 June 2024: it applies to 'affaires introduites à partir de sa date d'entrée en vigueur' (art. 41). An action introduced earlier stays under Titre 2 as the law of 28 March 2014 enacted it, under which the court chose the option system at admissibility (section 2). Every ruling this annex cites was given under that earlier text: by the Cour constitutionnelle, the Nederlandstalige rechtbank van eerste aanleg Brussel (Rb Brussel) and the Hof van beroep Brussel.

- **Who may sue.** Only a qualified entity (XVII.39 §1): an entity the minister has approved, a foreign entity on the Commission's list, or a legal person the court approves for the one action on the same criteria (XVII.1 §3). The criteria include twelve months of real public activity protecting consumers, a non-profit aim, and independence from those with an economic interest in the action, 'y compris en cas de financement par des tiers' (XVII.1 §1, 1°, 3° and 5°). The Consumer Mediation Service may represent the group in negotiation alone. The claimant's statutory object must be 'en relation directe avec l'objet de l'action en réparation collective' (XVII.36, 2°).
- **Court.** The courts of Brussels alone: 'Les cours et tribunaux de Bruxelles sont compétents pour connaître des actions en réparation collective' (XVII.35). The requête goes to the greffe of the tribunal de l'entreprise (XVII.42 §1).
- **Admissibility.** The judge tests the cause of the action, the claimant's standing and statutory object, and whether a collective action 'semble plus efficient qu'une action de droit commun' (XVII.36, 3°). The 2024 law struck out the condition that the claimant be 'jugé adéquat par le juge' (art. 20), since accreditation settles its aptitude: 'il ne peut être question de les soumettre à nouveau à un examen quant à leurs aptitudes' (exposé des motifs, DOC 55 3895/001, p. 26). No minimum number of members and no member list is asked. Where several claimants seek to represent one group, the judge appoints 'le plus adéquat d'entre eux' (XVII.40 §2).
- **The temporal limit.** The action lies only for harm from a common cause after the law of 28 March 2014 came into force on 1 September 2014: 'L'action en réparation collective ne peut être introduite que si la cause commune du dommage collectif s'est produite après l'entrée en vigueur de la présente loi' (art. 4 of that law, a transitional provision outside the CDE, which the 2024 law left standing). The Cour constitutionnelle upheld it: victims 'peuvent ou non recourir à l’action en réparation collective, selon que la cause commune de ce dommage s’est produite après ou avant cette date' (arrêt 41/2016, B.8.1), and 'Le premier moyen n’est pas fondé' (B.15). In VW the line ran through the class: Rb Brussel admitted the action only for owners of a vehicle 'aangekocht op en na 1 september 2014', and left earlier buyers to an action under the common law (Rb Brussel 18 December 2017, AR 2016/2706/A, dispositif and para. 14).

## 2. Who is bound, and how they join

- **After a merits judgment: opt-in, for everyone.** People harmed who want the benefit of a judgment that finds the defendant liable 'disposent d'un délai de quatre mois pour s'adresser au greffe pour rejoindre le groupe', counted from 'le lendemain de la publication au Moniteur belge de la décision du juge sur le fond' (XVII.55/1 §1). The judgment states the four months 'ainsi que les modalités à suivre' (XVII.54 §1, 8°). The choice is final: 'L'exercice du droit d'option par le consommateur et/ou la PME est irrévocable.'
- **In an approved settlement: the agreement chooses, within two limits.** The agreement sets 'le système d'option applicable au groupe' and the window for exercising the option 'auprès du greffe' (XVII.45 §3, 7°). People abroad always opt in: 'Pour les consommateurs qui ne résident pas de manière habituelle en Belgique et les PME qui n'ont pas leur établissement principal en Belgique, le système d'option d'inclusion est d'application.' So does everyone where the harm is bodily or moral: 'Si l'action vise à la réparation d'un préjudice collectif corporel ou moral, seul le système d'option d'inclusion est applicable'. For other harm the agreement may bind residents unless they opt out at the greffe. The judge refuses to approve an agreement whose window is 'manifestement déraisonnable' (XVII.49 §2), and the approval 'lie tous les membre du groupe' once they have exercised their option (XVII.49 §4).
- **Actions introduced before 10 June 2024.** The admissibility decision chose opt-in or opt-out and set a window of 30 days to three months (former XVII.43 §2, 3° and 7°), and 'Le consommateur communique son option au greffe' (former XVII.38 §1). In VW the court applied opt-out to residents, and 'voor de niet-verblijfhouders was automatisch het opt-in systeem van toepassing' (Rb Brussel 27 July 2023, AR 2016/2706/A, para. 13).
- **The greffe, not the register, holds the group.** Each member addresses the greffe of the Brussels court seised, or of the Hof van beroep on appeal. The liquidateur draws up the provisional list 'sur base des données que le greffe lui communique', and it holds the members 'qui se sont fait connaître expressément' (XVII.58 §1). The representative holds no list in law, and signing up with it is not joining the group: 'Men kan dus ook groepslid worden zonder aanmelding bij Test Aankoop.' (Rb Brussel 18 December 2017, para. 46). The register is the representative's evidence for its estimate of the group, and its intake for the options members lodge at the greffe.
- **Other proceedings.** A member who settles individually out of court leaves the group (XVII.68). A partie civile in criminal proceedings is a member only if it withdraws before the option deadline (XVII.67). Once a person is a member, identical individual proceedings lapse and new ones are inadmissible (XVII.69). No text read asks a member to declare that they claim nowhere else.
- **The export, in a Belgian action.** `be.json` sets `opt_in_abroad` and `opt_in_window` to true, names the greffe as the channel, `registry`, asks no declaration on parallel actions, and leaves it to the action whether residents opt in (`opt_in_residents`: `action`). The export states `opt_in_residents` as true after a merits judgment in an action introduced from 10 June 2024 (XVII.55/1 §1, inserted by art. 34 of the 2024 law), in a settlement that applies opt-in to residents or concerns bodily or moral harm (XVII.45 §3, 7°), and in an action introduced before 10 June 2024 whose admissibility decision chose opt-in; as false where residents are bound unless they opt out, in a settlement or under an earlier admissibility decision, whose choice holds after judgment too; and per subgroup where a settlement sets the option system per category. Until one of these fixes it, the export states `opt_in_residents` as true: with no term set, records count on their own facts either way (core 4.6). The export states the window as a term (`opt_in_terms`), from its first day: after a merits judgment, the day after its publication in the Moniteur belge; in a settlement, the day the approved agreement says it starts; in an action introduced before 10 June 2024, the day the admissibility decision says it starts. Its last day, after a merits judgment, is four months on. Until a window is set the export states no term, and a person abroad counts on their residence alone (core 4.6). No court tests a count of sign-ups (section 3), so whether support from abroad counts before the window closes does not arise. The term's channel stays null: the judgment or agreement sets the modalities, in VW a letter by hand or by registered post or a filing through e-Deposit, and each reaches the greffe, so the record names its channel `registry`.

## 3. When the register is tested

| Admissibility | Standing, statutory object and efficiency, the last judged on the potential group: the number of potential members, the size of each loss, the common cause. Sign-ups are no condition | XVII.36; Rb Brussel 4 April 2016 (Thomas Cook), paras. 20 and 31; Hof van beroep Brussel 10 January 2018 (Proximus), para. 35; Rb Brussel 18 December 2017 (VW), para. 41 |

| Distribution | The liquidateur's provisional list from the data of the greffe, contested entries with reasons, 30 days to contest at the greffe, a hearing, the final list | XVII.57 §1; XVII.58 §§ 1 to 5 |

**Reference dates.** The filing date, for the description and the estimate; the last day of the option window, for who joined; and the provisional and final lists at distribution (XVII.58 §§ 1 and 5). The SPF Economie, which publishes the decisions that admit an action, approved settlements and merits decisions, listed none given under the 2024 law on 19 October 2026.

## 5. Recommended items

- **A register before filing, with the method that links it to the estimate.** The efficiency test weighs the potential size of the group: 'de potentiële omvang van de groep van belang kan zijn bij de beoordeling van de derde ontvankelijkheidsvoorwaarde' (Rb Brussel 4 April 2016, 2015/4019/A, Thomas Cook, para. 20); 'Ten eerste is er in casu een groot aantal consumenten die potentieel schade hebben geleden.' (Hof van beroep Brussel 10 January 2018, 2017/AR/721, Proximus, para. 35). Identified members also answer a defendant who says the claims are paid: in Thomas Cook a passenger list 'met de namen van 183 personen' and payment lists did not show that every passenger had been paid (para. 29).
- **A count before filing that asks no further consent.** 'Niets belet Test Aankoop echter dat zij voorafgaand een peiling doet naar het aantal mogelijke gedupeerden, zonder dat men de consument vraagt in te stemmen met de opname van zijn persoonsgegevens in een databank die voor andere doeleinden dan de aanmelding wordt gebruikt.' (Rb Brussel 18 December 2017, para. 48).
- **Reconciliation with the list of the greffe.** The provisional list goes to the representative and 'peut être consultée au greffe' (XVII.58 §2). A register that marks which records the greffe holds shows the options lodged without it, which BE-4 needs, and how many sign-ups never joined. Few come forward: in VW nobody used the window the admissibility decision opened, 'Niemand oefende naar aanleiding van deze bekendmaking een keuzerecht uit' (Rb Brussel 27 July 2023, para. 13), and in Proximus 'seules 2000 personnes sur environ 30 000 personnes concernées se sont manifestées' (Conseil central de l'économie, avis CCE 2022-2910, p. 8, on figures from the consumer organisations).
- **A check against the defendant's own data.** Where the defendant 'a connaissance ou peut facilement avoir connaissance de l'identité des personnes lésées', it must inform them individually at its own cost (XVII.45 §3, 12°; XVII.54 §1, 6°). Where that list reaches the representative, a record checked against it rests on a source outside the person (core 4.3).
- **Other proceedings per record.** An individual settlement out of court ends membership (XVII.68), and so does a constitution as partie civile kept past the option deadline (XVII.67): the record turns `excluded`. Identical individual proceedings lapse once the person is a member (XVII.69), so the record notes them.
- **An export the greffe and the liquidateur can take in.** Per member the case reference, name, contact details, the claim and its documents, ready to file by hand, by registered post or through e-Deposit. The VW judgment asked for a letter with 'de referte van de huidige zaak', 'zijn naam en de contactgegevens', 'de vermelding dat hij aanspraak maakt op een vergoeding' and 'het aankoopbewijs, het betalingsbewijs en het inschrijvingsbewijs' (Rb Brussel 27 July 2023, dispositif).
- **The operator invoiced per action.** In a settlement the representative recovers from the defendant no more than 'les frais réels exposés par le représentant' (XVII.45 §3, 8°), so an operator invoice for this action alone lets the representative show its real costs.

## 6. Recognised identification

- **Identity, grade A**: notified eIDAS means. `be.json` names no further scheme.
- **Outside sources, grade B**: the defendant's own data, where it must inform members individually (section 5). The documents a judgment names, in VW the proof of purchase, payment and registration of the vehicle, are evidence the person supplies (core 4.3).

## 7. The level a typical action needs

A recommendation, not a court's rule.

- **Filing and admissibility: Level 1, Registered.** No court tests a count: the register supports the estimate of the group and the efficiency test. Even under the adequacy test since repealed, representativeness 'hangt in deze zaak echter niet af van het aantal mensen die zich reeds bij Test Aankoop, de enige groepsvertegenwoordiger, hebben aangemeld' (Rb Brussel 18 December 2017, para. 41).
- **Joining and distribution: Level 3, Verified**, where the register prepares the options members lodge at the greffe. The liquidateur contests each entry that does not fit the group, its sub-category or the prescribed modalities (XVII.58 §1), entries can be contested at the greffe within 30 days (XVII.58 §3), and the judgment sets documentary proof per member (BE-7), which a record at grade B, supplied, carries.

## 8. Unsettled

- **Whether the register can be the channel.** In VW the judgment named the greffe, by letter or through e-Deposit, yet the SPF Economie reports that 'Les demandes d'indemnisation peuvent être introduites via la plateforme en ligne de Test-Achats'. No source read states on what basis the platform stood in for the greffe, whether an approved agreement set it, or whether the greffe received the claims. Where a judgment or an approved agreement names a channel other than the greffe, the export states it as `opt_in_channel` and the conformance statement names the decision.
- **Options lodged for members.** No statute, decree or ruling read says whether a representative or an operator may lodge options at the greffe on behalf of members, one by one or in bulk. A written mandate from each member is the most plausible basis, and untested.
- **A royal decree on the channel.** 'Le Roi peut préciser les voies par lesquelles le consommateur et/ou la PME peut communiquer son choix au greffe' (XVII.55/1 §1). None appears among the executing decrees of the CDE that Justel listed on 21 October 2026. A decree would fix the channel BE-3 and BE-4 name.
- **Withdrawal in a settlement.** XVII.55/1 §1 makes an option after a merits judgment irrevocable; no text read says whether an option under an approved agreement can be withdrawn.
- **The first action under the 2024 law.** No admissibility decision under it has been published (section 3). It will be the first ruling on admissibility without the adequacy test and without a choice of option system.
- **A foundation as ad hoc entity.** Art. I.21, 6° defines the representative of the group as 'l'association qui agit au nom du groupe'; whether a foundation can be approved for one action is untested.
- **Consultation of the list.** XVII.58 §2 makes the provisional list consultable at the greffe without saying by whom.
- **No declaration on parallel actions.** No Belgian text read asks a member to declare that they are not represented elsewhere: XVII.67 to XVII.69 deal with other proceedings by ending membership or the other proceedings. `be.json` sets `parallel_declaration` to false, so the validator asks no declaration of a record abroad.
