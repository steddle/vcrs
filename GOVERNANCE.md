# Governance

## Who writes VCRS

Steddle drafts and maintains VCRS: Holly Thackwray and Mischa Sigtermans, for Steddle Group B.V., Amsterdam. Steddle builds and runs claimant registers for collective actions, so it has an interest in this standard. That interest is stated here and on every published version.

## What Steddle never does under VCRS

- Certify a register, or state that a register meets a level. An independent auditor checks a register (core, section 8) and the representative claims the level.
- Make conformance depend on Steddle. The text, the schemas and the vectors are free to use, and a representative with a spreadsheet, a notary and an auditor can meet Level 1 without any Steddle product.

## How the text is held to the law

- Every Required item in an annex cites the statute or the published ruling it rests on, with its paragraph, and quotes it in the original language. An item this standard requires beyond the law stands in a table of its own, with the case law that gives the reason.
- The core states no rule of law. It sets what a register must do to claim a level.
- An annex may tighten the core and never loosens it.

## Changes and versions

- Every change is public, in this repository.
- Versions follow Semantic Versioning, `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. Before 1.0, a change to a grade rule or a level's conditions raises the minor, and an editorial correction the patch. A pre-release such as `0.1.0-beta.1` is a draft for review.
- An export names the exact version it was produced under, and is checked against that version's schemas.
- Each released version is published with a persistent identifier, so a filing cites a text that does not change.
- `CHANGELOG.md` records what changed in each version.

## Consultation

- Comments arrive as issues in this repository, or by email to hello@steddle.com for anyone who prefers it. Each emailed comment is logged as an issue, with the sender's consent to be named or not.
- A draft goes out for consultation to foundations, funders, defendants' counsel, auditors and academics before it is released.
- Each annex names who wrote it and who reviewed it.

## The review panel

Once a review panel is seated, it approves changes to the standard text. No operator of claimant registers, Steddle included, holds a majority on it. Until it is seated, Steddle publishes each change for comment before a release.
